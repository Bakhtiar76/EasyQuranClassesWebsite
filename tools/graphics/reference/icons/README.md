# Icon reference crops

Every icon, brand mark and decorative motif appearing in the client's design
screenshots, cut out of the source JPEGs at native resolution. **Reference
only** — nothing here ships. The shipped icons are the SVG symbols in
`wp-content/themes/easy-quran-classes-child/inc/icon-sprite.php`, generated by
`tools/graphics/build-icon-sprite.mjs`.

Use these to check a shipped symbol against what the client actually drew,
and to see the geometry of the motifs that have no symbol yet.

- `contact-sheet.png` — all 98 crops on one page, labelled. Start here.
- `icons.json` — manifest: name, group, source file, exact source rect,
  pixel size, and where the icon appears in the design.
- `<name>.png` — one crop per icon, lossless, at the source's own scale.

## How they were cut

Crops are snapped to the icon's own ink, not to eye-measured boxes: each was
found by thresholding a generous search region, grouping connected components
that sit within a few pixels of each other (a calendar's dots, a chevron and
its ring, a rosette's petals are all one icon), and keeping the cluster
nearest the region centre. The threshold is anchored on each region's median
and scaled to its own tone range, because a 1px gold hairline on cream never
reaches full saturation once JPEG has had it, and a fixed tolerance sees
nothing there at all.

Five elements are hand-measured instead, recorded in `icons.json` as
`"polarity": "manual"` — the footer seal, the pricing medallion, the teacher
medallion, the course number badge and the testimonial quote badge. Each
either straddles another shape (the seal sits astride the dark footer bar) or
is drawn in a tone too faint to separate from the card behind it (the number
badge's scallop). No amount of thresholding separates those, so they were
measured off a grid overlay.

The `bbox` in `icons.json` is the exact source rectangle, so any crop can be
re-cut or widened from the original JPEG without repeating the search.

idea two ways, both are kept — `graduation-cap` (Teachers, small),

`people-pair`, `shield-star`).

`Website Layout.png` contributed no crops: it is a 992px-wide full-page mock
in which every icon is a low-fidelity ~60% reduction of the same icon in the
section screenshots. It stays useful for section order, not for geometry.

## Audit against the shipped sprite — and what came of it

Every crop was rendered side by side with the symbol the theme shipped for it,
at matched size, and judged by looking. The sprite went from 43 symbols to 57
as a result. `tools/graphics/scratch/audit-icons.mjs` regenerates those
comparison sheets at any time.

**Confirmed already correct, left untouched:** `person-filled`,
`users-filled` (the two-figure pair), `shield-filled`, `quote-filled`,
`map-pin-filled`, `chevrons-right`, `chevron-right`, and the Simple Icons
brand marks `twitter`, `instagram`, `youtube`, `whatsapp`.

**Was wrong, now redrawn** (`ORIGINAL_OUTLINE` / `FILLED_UI` in
`build-icon-sprite.mjs`): `calendar` lacked the design's 2×3 date dots;
`chart-up` was the trend arrow without its bars; `headset` had no boom mic;
`globe` carried one latitude instead of three; `certificate` had no text lines
and a ribbon rather than a medal; `megaphone`, `clock` (no dial ticks),
`star-filled` (too thin), `book-open` (too round), `graduation-cap` (no crown
band), `facebook` (the design uses the bare `f`, not the f-in-a-circle brand
mark), and `certificate-filled`, whose medal had a hollow centre where the
design knocks out a star.

**Replaced again by client-supplied artwork:** `graduation-cap-filled`,
`graduate`, `people-pair`, `rehal-quran`, `presenter` and `target-arrow` were
first drawn here from primitives and read poorly at size — the composed
silhouettes were awkward in a way the reference crops are not. The client
supplied proper vector artwork for exactly those six; it is now the source
(`tools/graphics/source-icons/`) and the drawn versions are gone.

**Newly drawn, nothing shipped before:** `users` (three-figure group),
`people-pair`, `presenter`, `graduate`, `shield-halved`, `shield-star`,
`clipboard-check`, `target-arrow`, `rehal-quran`, `quote-bubble`,
`rosette-star`, plus filled variants `book-open-filled`, `arrow-right-filled`,
`sparkle-filled` and solid `phone` / `mail`.

Two corrections to an earlier draft of this file, which was written before the
symbols were rendered and compared:

- **`certificate-person` did have a near-equivalent.** `certificate-filled`
  was already an award rosette with ribbon tails; only its centre was wrong.
  Calling it "no equivalent" overstated the gap.
- **The crop named `shield-check` is not a check.** It is a shield inside a
  shield with the inner one halved. The crop keeps its filename so links stay
  valid, but the symbol it maps to is `shield-halved`.

Not a gap: `chevron-left` has no symbol of its own, but the design's previous
buttons are `chevron-right` rotated 180°, which is exactly what
`components.css` already does (`.eqc-nav-btn:first-child .eqc-icon`).

**Ornament gaps resolved 2026-09-09.** The generic geometric-star reuse and
the AI reference's excessive flower variation were replaced by one coherent
two-level family in `gen-ornaments.mjs`: `rosette-reviews.svg` is the layered
signature motif and `rosette-simple.svg` is its reduced outline. Curved
four-point sparkles replace the old
hollow pricing diamond; the course number badge is an unfilled scalloped
outline; pricing bullets/benefit frames are scalloped; and the pricing
medallion's cream/gold outer shell is derived from the approved alphabet
cartouche contour. All page builders were then re-run with `--user=1` and
Elementor CSS was flushed. See `QA/GRAPHICS-PARITY.md` and
`QA/icon-parity/collage-14.png` for the mapping and final comparison.
